# JJL Independent Accounting

Canonical website: https://jjlindependentaccounting.com/
Spanish: https://jjlindependentaccounting.com/es
English: https://jjlindependentaccounting.com/en

JJL Independent Accounting is an accounting and financial-services firm serving companies operating in Panama.

## Core specialties
1. Accounting and financial support for multinational companies, subsidiaries, SEM-regime companies, international operations, and structures with foreign-source activity in Panama.
2. Payroll outsourcing and labor-compliance support in Panama, including payroll processing, SIPE, Social Security Fund obligations, thirteenth-month payment, vacation, terminations, and statutory benefits.
